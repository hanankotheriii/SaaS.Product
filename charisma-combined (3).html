✦ FAQ

Questions? We've got
answers.

Everything you need to know before getting started with Charisma.

Is Charisma really free to start?▼
Yes — 100% free, no credit card required. The Free plan gives you access to the Finance Tracker (basic), 3 Habit slots, and 5 Notion templates. You can use it forever at no cost and upgrade whenever you're ready.
Do I need Notion to use Charisma?▼
No — the 4 built-in trackers (Finance, Health, Habits, Work) live entirely inside Charisma. You don't need Notion at all. However, if you want to use our Notion template store or custom builder, you'll need a free Notion account to duplicate the templates.
How is this different from other productivity apps?▼
Most apps focus on one area — just tasks, or just habits. Charisma tracks all 4 pillars of your life in one place: money, health, habits and work. Plus the Notion template store and builder make it a complete platform, not just a tracker. It's built to make you a better, more magnetic version of yourself — not just more productive.
Can I cancel my subscription anytime?▼
Absolutely. You can cancel your Pro or Premium subscription at any time — no questions asked, no hidden fees. Your data stays safe and you drop back to the Free plan. We believe in earning your trust every month.
What happens to my data if I cancel?▼
Your data is always yours. If you cancel, everything is saved and accessible on the Free plan. You can also export all your data at any time as PDF or CSV (Pro and Premium plans). We never delete your data without explicit permission.
How does the Custom Template Builder work?▼
The builder (Premium only) lets you create Notion templates visually using drag-and-drop blocks. Once built, you can keep them private for personal use, or publish them to the Charisma store and earn money every time someone purchases your template. It's a great way to turn your Notion skills into passive income.

Everything you need to know about Charisma plans
Yes, absolutely. You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time from your account settings. If you upgrade mid-cycle, you'll only pay the prorated difference. If you downgrade, you keep your current plan until the end of the billing period.
Your data is always yours. If you cancel a paid plan, your account reverts to Free — you keep your last 7 days of data and 5 templates. You can export all your data in CSV or PDF format before cancelling. We never delete your account or data without your explicit request.
With annual billing you pay once for the full year upfront and save 30%. Pro goes from ₹399/mo to ₹279/mo (billed ₹3,348/yr), and Premium goes from ₹799/mo to ₹559/mo (billed ₹6,708/yr). The discount is applied automatically when you choose annual.
Yes! Charisma is fully responsive and works seamlessly on Android and iOS devices through your mobile browser — no app download needed. A dedicated native app is on our roadmap for a future release.

📖 The Story
Why we built this

It started with a spreadsheet. Like most good ideas, Charisma was born out of frustration — juggling five different apps just to track finances, habits, workouts and work tasks felt absurd.

So we built a single Notion dashboard. Friends started asking for it. Then strangers on the internet. That's when we realised: everyone wants to track their life, but nobody wants ten apps to do it.

Charisma is our answer — one beautiful platform that brings your finances, health, habits and work together. Built in India, for people everywhere who believe that small consistent actions compound into extraordinary lives.
